The project

A closer look.

I built the EduOak Consulting website using WordPress, with Canva used for visual assets. The project brings the consultancy’s education services into one place, helping prospective students explore their options and find a clear route to getting in touch.

The website introduces the consultancy and organizes information around the questions students have when planning further study: which program to choose, where to study, what preparation is needed, and how to begin an application. Its green-and-white branding connects the service pages, study destinations, and enquiry options.

What the website includes

  • Education services: Information on career counselling, admission guidance, applications, scholarships, and pre-departure preparation.
  • Study options: Dedicated navigation for foundation, undergraduate, postgraduate, and distance-learning programs, alongside overseas study destinations.
  • Test preparation: A section introducing preparation options for exams such as IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, GRE, GMAT, and SAT.
  • Student enquiries: Contact links and an appointment form where visitors can share their study preferences and request guidance.
  • Brand presentation: WordPress page layouts and Canva visuals that bring the consultancy’s services and educational content together.

My contribution

Website development in WordPress and visual asset creation with Canva, delivered as a freelance project. The live site is linked above so you can explore the pages and see the work in context.
