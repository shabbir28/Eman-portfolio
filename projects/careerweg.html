The project

A closer look.

CareerWeg is a modern career platform designed to support both job seekers and employers through a seamless digital experience. It brings profile building, job discovery, hiring workflows, and learning opportunities together in one place—creating an ecosystem for career growth and recruitment.

Users can sign up as students or employers, create personalized profiles, and manage professional details such as education, skills, experience, languages, portfolio links, and contact information. Job seekers can explore opportunities, apply for roles, and follow their career progress, while employers can post and manage listings and review applicants.

One platform for career growth

  • Personalized profiles: Student and employer accounts bring education, skills, experience, languages, portfolio links, and contact details together.
  • Job discovery and hiring: Job seekers can find and apply for roles, while employers can publish listings and review applicants.
  • Learning resources: Courses, career roadmaps, skill tests, and certificates support ongoing development and qualifications.
  • Career progress: The platform connects learning and job opportunities in a single experience for continued career growth.

More than a hiring portal, CareerWeg is designed as a career companion: it supports professional development while helping employers discover and connect with talent.

Technology

Developed as my final-year Software Engineering project, CareerWeg combines a dynamic frontend interface with Java and Spring Boot services backed by MySQL. The project brings together a user-friendly experience and a full-stack foundation for recruitment and learning workflows.
